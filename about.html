01 / My story

I've spent more than 8 years in digital: as a marketer, head of marketing and web project manager, from fintech and e-commerce to wellness and NGOs.

I studied in Ljubljana, Scotland, Canada, Turkey and Uruguay, and worked in Spain, Egypt, Czechia and Bolivia. I have a master's in international business.

Today, as an independent marketer, I take over marketing for small businesses part-time. I combine data with intuition and strategy with execution, and I always know what we measure and why.
