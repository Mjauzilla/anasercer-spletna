SpinaliS: faster content, visible in ChatGPT.

Challenge

Approach

I set up an AI workflow for articles and content: AI prepares a draft, I edit it into the brand's voice and optimise it for Google and AI search.

08 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

Will the content sound like a robot?+

No. AI prepares a draft and I edit it into your voice. Nothing goes out without a human review.

Which AI tools do you use?+

ChatGPT and Claude for copy, Midjourney and Kling for visuals and video, Zapier and Make for automation. I choose tools based on your needs and budget.

Do I need technical knowledge?+

No. I set everything up and write you simple instructions.

Is AI content bad for SEO?+

Bad content is bad for SEO, whoever writes it. Good, reviewed and specific content works, even if AI helped write it.

What about data privacy?+

I don't put your customer data into public AI tools. For sensitive data I use private settings or anonymise it.

How long does setup take?+

The audit takes one week, setup 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the number of processes.

Who does AI marketing in Slovenia?+

Ana Šercer, a digital marketer from Ljubljana with more than 8 years of experience, who uses AI tools for content, visuals, automation and GEO for Slovenian businesses.
