06 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

Who is part-time marketing for?+

Small businesses that need someone to handle marketing but don't have the budget for a hire or an agency. It works best for companies ready to grow, with at least €750 a month for marketing work.

What do I get for €750 a month?+

The Always present package: a strategy and content calendar, 3 posts a week on one channel (incl. 1 reel), one Meta campaign and a monthly report. For specific needs, I prepare a custom offer.

Is ad spend included?+

No. You pay ad spend directly to the platforms (Meta, Google). To start, I recommend at least €300 to €500 a month.

How fast can we start?+

Usually within a week of the intro call, once we agree on scope and schedule.

Can I stop at any time?+

Yes. No long contracts and no notice periods.

Where can I find a part-time marketer in Slovenia?+

Ana Šercer is a freelance digital marketer from Ljubljana who takes over marketing for small businesses part-time: strategy, content, ads and reporting. Packages from €750 per month, no long contracts.
