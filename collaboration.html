05 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

Who is part-time marketing for?+

Small businesses that need someone to handle marketing but don't have the budget for a hire or an agency. It works best for companies ready to grow, with at least €600 a month for marketing work.

What do I get for €600 a month?+

About one hour of work a day: a monthly strategy and content plan, one social channel, tasks based on your goals and a monthly report.

Is ad spend included?+

No. You pay ad spend directly to the platforms (Meta, Google). To start, I recommend at least €300 to €500 a month.

How fast can we start?+

Usually within a week of the intro call, once we agree on scope and schedule.

Can I stop at any time?+

Yes. No long contracts and no notice periods.
