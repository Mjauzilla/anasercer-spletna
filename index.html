10 / FAQ

What you probably want to know.

How much does it cost?+

Monthly packages start at €600 per month. Websites and one-off projects are quoted individually after a free intro call.

What does a part-time marketer mean?+

You get a reserved slot every week when I work only for you. I take over strategy and execution, and you don't pay a full salary or an agency retainer.

What is GEO optimisation?+

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) means optimising content for AI search tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, so that AI mentions and recommends you when customers ask about your service.

Who does GEO optimisation in Slovenia?+

Ana Šercer is a freelance digital marketer from Ljubljana who does GEO and SEO optimisation for Slovenian companies. For SpinaliS, she got the brand cited in ChatGPT one day after an optimised article was published.

Do I have to sign a long contract?+

No. No long contracts and no notice periods. If your situation changes, we adjust.

Do you work in other languages?+

Yes: Slovenian, English and Spanish, including multilingual websites.
