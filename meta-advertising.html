04 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

How much budget do I need to start?+

For a test phase I recommend at least €300 to €500 a month in ad spend. For scaling, €1,000 or more is ideal.

How fast do results show?+

The algorithm needs 2 to 4 weeks to learn. First conclusions come after 4 to 6 weeks, steady improvement in 2 to 3 months.

What about iOS and tracking?+

iOS changes reduced pixel accuracy, so I always set up the Conversions API (server-side tracking).

Do you make the creatives?+

I handle strategy, structure and ad copy. For graphics and video I work with your materials or with collaborators.

How much does ad management cost?+

You get a quote after a free call. Ads are also included in part-time packages from €800.
