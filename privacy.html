Privacy
and cookies.

Short and clear: what data I collect, why, and how you stay in control.

Who looks after your data

The data controller is Ana Šercer s.p., Ljubljana, Slovenia. For any question about your data, write to anasercer@gmail.com or call +386 68 617 822.

What I collect and why

  • When you write or call: your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. I use them only to reply and prepare an offer.
  • When you book a call: your name, email address and any answers you give in the form. Bookings run through Calendly, which also sends you the confirmation.
  • When we work together: the data needed to do the agreed work and to issue an invoice.
  • When you visit the site and allow cookies: visit data (pages viewed, a click on the booking button, a completed booking), collected by the Meta pixel and the Google Ads tag.

I do not sell your data or use it for anything other than the above.

Cookies

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. The Meta pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and the Google Ads measurement tag load only after you click "Allow" in the notice. I use it to measure how many visitors come from ads and how many book a call, and to show ads to people who have already visited the site. Meta cookies are kept for up to 90 days.

Your browser remembers your choice locally. You can change it at any time: change cookie settings.

The booking window loads Calendly, which uses its own essential cookies to make the booking work.

Who else processes data

  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. for measuring and showing ads (only with your consent).
  • Google Ireland Ltd. (Google Ads) for measuring ad performance (only with your consent).
  • Calendly LLC for booking appointments.
  • Google for email and calendar.
  • The web hosting provider that runs the site and keeps technical access logs (IP address, time of visit) for security.

Some providers are based in the USA. Data is transferred under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Legal basis and retention

  • Enquiries and bookings: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract. Kept while we are in talks or working together, and for at most two years after our last contact.
  • Invoices and business records: legal obligation. Kept for 10 years.
  • Meta pixel: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see your data, have it corrected, deleted, restricted or transferred, and you can object to processing. A message to anasercer@gmail.com is enough, and I reply within one month.

If you think I am not handling your data correctly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (ip-rs.si).
