05 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

What is SEO optimisation?+

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is improving a website's visibility on Google. It includes technical optimisation, a keyword-based content strategy and building authority.

How long until I see results?+

Realistically 3 to 6 months. Google picks up technical fixes sooner; content authority builds over time.

How much does SEO cost?+

It depends on scope. You get a quote after a free 30-minute call. SEO is also included in part-time packages from €800.

Do you write the content yourself?+

Yes. Writing is one of my strengths: articles, landing pages and service pages.

What about link building?+

I focus on natural mentions through quality content and relevant media, which is safer than buying links.

Do SEO and GEO work together?+

Yes. Good SEO content is a great foundation for GEO, so one piece of work covers Google and AI search.
