04 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

Which platforms do you manage?+

Mostly Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. TikTok when it makes sense.

How many posts per month?+

It depends on your goals and budget. Often 8 to 12 posts a month, a mix of carousels, reels and stories.

Do I need to prepare anything?+

Only the photos and footage you already have, and approving the posts. I prepare everything else.

Can we combine social media and ads?+

Of course. Organic posts and Meta ads work best together, and both are included in the part-time packages.

How much does it cost?+

You get a quote after a free call. Social media is also included in the Part-time marketer packages from €600 per month.
