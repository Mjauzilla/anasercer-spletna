05 / FAQ

Questions and answers.

How long does it take?+

A simple site is ready in 1 to 3 weeks, a multilingual e-commerce site in 4 to 8 weeks.

Can I edit the site myself?+

Yes. After handover you get short instructions for editing content.

Which platform is best?+

WordPress for content-rich sites and SEO, Shopify for serious e-commerce, custom HTML for fast portfolio sites. We choose together.

Is SEO included?+

Yes, every site has basic SEO: clean URLs, titles and descriptions, speed and mobile-friendliness. In-depth SEO is a separate service.

What about hosting and domain?+
